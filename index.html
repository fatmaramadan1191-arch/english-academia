About Your Teacher

I’m Mrs. Fatma Ramadan, and I have been teaching English for over 10 years, working across British and international curricula.

Working as a teacher and tutor has given me a valuable insight into both the classroom and the examination process. It has helped me understand what students need to succeed and how to support them in achieving their academic goals, whether they are preparing for IGCSE, Cambridge Checkpoint, or IB English.

I am passionate about making English clear, practical, and accessible. I also enjoy creating high-quality educational resources that support students in their learning and help teachers deliver effective, engaging lessons.

Teaching. Examining. Creating. Inspiring.
